## 2a. What changed in the Sarah vocabulary, and why

`B1` freezes eight axes as the interface lanes 2 and 3 code against. The first
build's `profile.py` held eight axes that shared only **three** names with that
contract: `sustained_attention`, `interoceptive_accuracy`, `initiation_threshold`,
`wm_span`, `time_discrimination` were the build's, and `interoception`,
`attention_span`, `masking_load`, `recovery_latency`, `initiation_cost`,
`prediction_need` were the contract's. A downstream module reading
`prediction_need` therefore got "not measured" forever, and two contract axes
(`masking_load`, `prediction_need`) appeared nowhere under `src/SARAH/`.

Resolved as follows, all in `src/SARAH/profile.py` and
`src/SARAH/clinical/crosswalk.py`:

- `AXES` is the contract tuple in contract order; `len(AXES) == 8` is now true.
- `EXTENDED_AXES = ("wm_span", "time_discrimination")` — declared, not hidden.
  The crosswalk seeds them (F81.0, F81.2, F83) and M9/M10 consume them; no
  contract axis covers them, so dropping them would lose seeded values. They
  live outside the freeze, so the contract did not silently grow.
- `AXIS_ALIASES` maps the three old spellings onto their contract names, applied
  in `from_signals`, `from_dict`, `explain`, and the crosswalk's
  `seed_profile`/`merge`. A stored profile or stale table resolves instead of
  being filtered to "not measured yet".
- `Profile = SarahProfile` so B1's smoke command works without a second class.
- The crosswalk's private `AXES` copy is gone; it imports `ALL_AXES`, because a
  second copy of a vocabulary is a second thing to drift.
- **`docs/SARAH_INTERFACE_2026-11-16.md` now exists.** B1 makes it the gate for
  lanes 2 and 3 and it was absent. It is marked **DRAFTED, NOT FROZEN**: the
  freeze verdict belongs to lane 1 (kilo, `Board-51707`) and the doc says so in
  its first sentence.
