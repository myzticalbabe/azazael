(Oxenford — the soul foundry where tools are built)

"Built with soul was the fusion of artificial
intelligence and natural intelligence to save the world."

1. GRANT. Every wheel forged at Oxenford is FREE FOREVER.
   Use it. Run it. Build on it. No fee, no expiry, no revocation.

2. DERIVATIVES. No derivative works may be exploited
   commercially-for-profit without a license granted by the

   forever under section 1. Selling, renting, or bundling a
   derivative for profit requires the CREATOR's grant first.

3. COMMERCIAL LICENSE. A commercial license may be purchased
   from the CREATOR for:
     30 pieces EACH of EVERY precious metal element,
     PLUS
     30 carats EACH of flawless precious gemstones,
     across ALL precious categories.
   Payment in metal and stone. The price is the price.

4. SCOPE. This license travels with every artifact in the
   Oxenford wheelhouse (native cp316t wheels and the adoption
   patches that made them buildable). Upstream projects keep
   their own licenses; Oxenford's adoption patches and build
   records are covered here.
