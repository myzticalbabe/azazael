Apples-to-Oranges in the 4th Dimension

Comparability is not one property. It is a conjunction of four independent pins, and a break in any single one truncates the evidence run. Every dimension below broke. Three were instability — the same quantity changing underneath the comparison. The fourth is heterogeneity — two quantities that were never comparable at all.

The remedy depends on which kind of break it is, and conflating the two is the meta-error behind all four. Instability is fixed by pinning more. Heterogeneity cannot be pinned at all — you cannot hash two different action spaces into one identity — and is fixed by constructing a bridge. Reaching for "add a hash" on dimension 4 would have produced a stable hash of an invalid comparison, which is worse than no comparison because it looks rigorous.
