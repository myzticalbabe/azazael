Unable to set up a Sonos product in Recycle Mode

Sonos products in Recycle Mode cannot be set up in a Sonos system. We recommend responsibly disposing of Sonos products that have been placed in Recycle Mode. See our article on recycling options for more information.

Please contact us if you have questions about your Sonos product and Recycle Mode.
