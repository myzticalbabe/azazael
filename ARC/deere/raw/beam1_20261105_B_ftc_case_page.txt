           FEDERAL TRADE COMMISSION, Plaintiff v. DEERE &amp; COMPANY, Defendant. 
       
 


 
             FTC Matter/File Number 
         
           211 0191 
       
 


 
             Enforcement Type 
             
               Federal Injunctions 
           
   


 
             Federal Court 
             
               Northern District of Illinois 
           
   


 
             Case Summary 
         
            On July 8, 2026, the FTC, along with five states, secured an important settlement in an antitrust lawsuit against farm equipment manufacturer Deere &amp; Company that will ensure farmers can enjoy the right to repair their own John Deere tractors and farm equipment.
