## 1. Gaps found

### E1 — the NSGA-II refine ran on cycles that could not use it
The refine was 1066s of the batch's 1177s (90.6%). A single live cycle localises
it: `promotion standing` at 23:25:48, `multi-objective:` at 23:26:05 — **17s in
the refine**, while the entire GA (`gen 0` .. `converged at gen 6`) is under 1s.
Its `front`/`knee` are only read inside the `beats and sprt_ok` block, so on
**33 of 51** cycles the search ran to completion and was discarded.

### E2 — "SPRT rejected" was recorded where the test never ran
`_sprt_decide()` is only reached under `if beats:`. The default was
`(0.0, 0.0, 0, 'reject')`, so a cycle the *margin* gate failed was stored and
displayed as an SPRT rejection with `n=0`. 31 of 51 cycles are that case, which
is why the old headline `{'reject': 31}` overstated the gate's decisiveness.

### E3 — the search validates rather than explores; `MIN_GENS` is the real stop
`gens = {6: 49, 7: 1, 8: 1}` and 38/51 flat best-trajectories. The live log shows
`gen 2`..`gen 5` as the *same* genome, then the early-stop firing as soon as
`MIN_GENS = 6` allows. The population is seeded from last-best + HOF + TPE + QD
elite, so it starts at/near the champion and has no gradient to climb; per-gen
improvement is ~0 by construction. **Still open after the widening** — flat
trajectories rose to 42/50, confirming this is seeding/elitism, not bounds.

### E4 — pool-era churn makes multi-observation evidence impossible
28 distinct pool eras in 51 cycles, ids up to 64 (~1.8 cycles/era).
`_sprt_history()` and `_compute_streak()` are pinned to `(pool_id,
baseline_hash)` and hard-stop at the first mismatch, while the SPRT needs 2..6
same-era observations. Still present (28 eras in the widened batch too), but no
longer binding, because the widened margins clear the gate on the first
observation.

### E5 — candidate margins clustered just under the gate while the baseline drifted
Pre-widen margins split: first 19 cycles at 1.87-2.51, remaining 32 at 0.00-0.46
(median 0.385) against `PROMOTE_MARGIN = 0.75` — a systematic ~0.37 shortfall
with very low dispersion. **Resolved by the widening** (median now 3.3012).

### E6 — the holdout gate is the terminal blocker
A live cycle cleared margin, SPRT and the pareto floor, then stopped at
`holdout: passed False` (cand 22.7275 vs base 22.9975). Post-widen this is now
the *only* gate that ever blocks (4 cycles).

### E7 — the second arm has been losing
Pre-widen `{held: 32, skipped: 19}` at 2.0% of wall clock, and `DE arm: held
(de=20.4526 ga=22.1713)` — the arm lost. Post-widen it is skipped **50/50**.

### E8 — the genome was pinned to a vertex of its own box (root cause of E3/E5)
`state/evolved_rl_params.json` (promoted 2026-09-11T14:05:31, seed 49909,
fitness 25.0425):

| Gene | Value | Old bound | Position |
|---|---|---|---|
| `learning_rate` | **0.5000** | (0.01, **0.5**) | exactly on the upper bound |
| `discount` | **0.8033** | (**0.8**, 0.999) | within 0.003 of the floor |
| `epsilon_decay` | **0.0859** | (0.0, **0.1**) | against 86% of a 0.1 ceiling |
| `epsilon` | 0.2960 | (0.0, 0.5) | the only genuinely free gene |

The QD archive's global best cell held the **identical** genome at the same
25.0425, so the archive's best point was also a pinned vertex.

With three of four genes against a wall, the effective search space was ~1-D and
its optimum was already occupied by production. That is why every era
re-converged to the same corner and margins clustered just under the gate.

**"Add more genes" is mostly a trap here.** `learning_rl.py:335` documents that
`n_episodes`/`max_steps` *"would create fake selection pressure via more
Q-updates"*, and `max_steps` is literally `range(min(max_steps, len(records)))`
(`:442`). The only genuine extra knob reachable without touching the trainer is
`use_schema_features` (a state-representation switch). Anything further requires
parameterizing internals that are hardcoded today: reward shaping, Q-table init,
forgetting, action weighting.

### E9 — a snapshot-pool margin no longer predicts holdout success (new)
Post-widen, **all 50** cycles beat the frozen snapshot baseline by +2.91 to
+3.39, and SPRT promoted all 50. Of the 4 cycles that reached the fresh holdout,
**all 4 failed**. So a +3.3 margin on the reused snapshot bought zero confirmed
improvement. The margin is measured on a frozen, reused pool that the GA can
overfit; the holdout is the only fresh-pool measurement and it disagrees
completely. Treat snapshot margin as a *search* signal, never as evidence of
deployment-quality improvement.

### E10 — the second arm is now permanently starved (new)
The arm gate is `_gate_margin < 2 * PROMOTE_MARGIN` (= 1.5). With widened bounds
every margin is >= 2.91, so the arm was skipped **50/50** — CMA/DE/TPE now
contribute nothing, and the ~11% arm cost is no longer the issue; the arm is
simply unreachable. The threshold needs to scale with the observed margin
distribution rather than a fixed multiple of the promotion gate.

## 2. Fixes applied (verified)

**E8 — widened the three bounds the search was pressed against**
(`scripts/evolution_cycle.py`):

| Gene | Old | New | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| `learning_rate` | 0.01 - 0.5 | 0.01 - **0.9** | production sat exactly on 0.5. Kept below 1.0: tabular Q-learning is only guaranteed to converge for a step size in (0, 1] |
| `discount` | **0.8** - 0.999 | **0.5** - 0.999 | production sat within 0.003 of the floor |
| `epsilon_decay` | 0.0 - 0.1 | 0.0 - **0.3** | production pressed a 0.1 ceiling |

Measured effect: run 1 of the post-change batch produced
`learning_rate 0.7073`, `discount 0.5613` — both **outside the old box** — and
took 10 generations where the old batch stopped at 6.

`production_genes()`'s range guard now checks against `GENE_BOUNDS` instead of
duplicated literals; the old literals would have silently rejected a genome
promoted onto a newly-widened bound and fallen back to `ScheduleConfig`
defaults. Verified: the existing pinned genome still loads from the file, so the
baseline stays comparable. The monitor's PIN check was synced to the same
numbers (its literals would otherwise have flagged a false PIN).

**E1 — gated the refine on the decision that consumes it**
```python
pareto = (_pareto_refine(evo, info_pool, alert_pool)
          if (beats and sprt_ok and best) else None)
```
Verified: forcing `beats=False` gives **3.0s** with no `multi-objective:` line,
versus ~20s when the refine runs.

**E2 — never-ran default is now distinguishable**
```python
sprt_llr, sprt_sigma, sprt_n, sprt_decision = 0.0, 0.0, 0, 'not_run'
```
Only `'promote'` is consumed as a pass anywhere; `'reject'` was matched for
colour only, so `'not_run'` renders amber (accurate) instead of red.

**Monitor** (`scripts/live_monitor.py`) — the wallboard hardcoded the arm label
`cma` while the secondary alternates `cma`/`de` by 5-min slot
(`_second_arm_name`), so DE duels were mislabelled and invisible; the duel is now
split per arm. Added the pareto->holdout funnel and the `not_run` gate state.

**Caveat on E1 in this batch:** `pareto_refine` still shows 93.4% of wall clock
(948s of 1015s), because the widening made *every* cycle clear the margin gate,
so the guard always permits the refine. The fix is correct but unexercised in
this regime — it only pays off on non-beating cycles, which no longer occur.

## 3. Recommended next (in priority order)

1. **E9 first.** A +3.3 snapshot margin that never survives holdout means the
   snapshot pool is being overfitted. Either shorten the pool reuse window, hold
   out a slice the GA never sees, or stop treating snapshot margin as evidence.
   Without this, widening the bounds bought search progress, not real gains.
2. **E10.** Scale the arm gate to the observed margin distribution, or the
   second search arm is dead.
3. **E3.** Make early-stop judge *population* movement, not the elite's — an
   elite frozen by elitism always looks converged (42/50 flat after widening).
4. **E4.** Anchor the evidence window to `baseline_hash` alone, or require an
   era to be stable before it counts.
5. **E6.** The holdout is now the sole gate; instrument *why* it fails.
